# Keep original papers available for cutting

## What will change
- Preserve a permanent reference to the uploaded full question paper and full answer key when homework is saved.
- Make **Cut answer** open the answer key even when questions and answers were uploaded together in one document.
- Make **Cut question** and every later **Recut** reopen the retained original full pages, rather than depending on another successful crop.
- Keep the full source pages private to teachers; students continue seeing only the selected question crop.

## Technical details
- Store source paper and answer-page references with each saved question.
- Return those references when reopening the homework editor and pass them into the cutting window.
- Let answer cutting fall back to pages from a combined paper/mark-scheme upload when there is no separate answer-key file.
- Preserve the existing fine crop controls and image-only question/answer behavior.
- Verify a missing answer crop can open full pages, be saved, then reopened for recutting.
